The real problem

Nobody logs the transfers.

Goods move between warehouses all day. Logging each move feels like extra work with nothing to show for it, so it gets skipped. The numbers drift, and soon nobody trusts them.

Stacko's answer: make logging take about 10 seconds, and make every log visible on the map as a truck, a ship or a plane.

Imports

From any port to your shelf.

Every import is tracked as one transfer: by sea or by air, through customs, then on a truck to the warehouse. Wherever your supplier is, you always know which leg it's on.

Drag to move the shipment along its route. Transit times are examples; in Stacko you set the ETA for each shipment or each leg.

Upload the invoice. Stacko builds the shipment.

Send the supplier's invoice or packing list as a PDF, an Excel file or a photo. Stacko creates the items and the incoming shipment for you.

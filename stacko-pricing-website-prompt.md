## About Stacko

Stacko (ستاكو) is a stock-management app with AI built in, made by AIPHIN. It shows every warehouse, shop and shipment on one live map, and teams can log stock moves by chat. It works in two ways:
- **As your full inventory app**, for companies without a system.
- **On top of the system you already use** (Odoo, ERPNext, Zoho Inventory, WooCommerce, Shopify, or an Excel/Google Sheets file). Stacko syncs with it, shows it on the map, and lets the AI work with it.

**Audience:** trading and distribution companies in Lebanon and the GCC with several warehouses or shops and regular imports.

## The pricing model (explain this simply on the page)

- **You pay per location.** A location is a warehouse, branch or shop. That's the only thing that sets the price.
- **Every plan includes every feature.** Plans differ only in size.
- **Users, connections and AI are included**, up to each plan's limits.
- **Same price either way:** using Stacko as your full app or on top of your current system costs the same.
- **Staff who only use the Telegram bot are free** and never count as users.
- **AI credits:** 1 credit = one question to the AI assistant, one Telegram message, or one document page read by AI. They reset every month.
- **Your own Claude (AI) key:** companies that connect one get unlimited AI that doesn't use their credits.
